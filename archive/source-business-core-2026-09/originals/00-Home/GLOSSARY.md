# Glossary

## Purpose

This file owns shared definitions. It does not own operational status, current pricing, or client transactions.

- **Canonical truth:** the single authoritative source for what is true now.
- **Mutable truth:** a current fact that may change and must be fresh-read from its canonical source.
- **Historical snapshot:** a transaction or event fact fixed to its original context and unaffected by later policy changes.
- **Derived view:** a display of facts owned elsewhere; never a source of truth.
- **Research:** unresolved, non-canonical material that may inform a later decision.
- **Client:** a persistent business relationship or entity.
- **Project:** the user-facing and operating-interface name for the existing Cycle entity; it is not a separate business entity, record, ID, status, or folder type.
- **Cycle:** the canonical entity and record for one independent Project or engagement for a Client.
- **Phase:** a stage within one Cycle and the owner of phase-level transactional state.
- **Decision Record:** the durable explanation for a material change to policy, offer, process, ICP, market wedge, ownership, or architecture.
- **Assessment:** an optional initial qualification step.
- **Discovery:** a lean conversation that captures decision-relevant information.
- **Focused Audit:** a diagnostic and recommendation engagement focused on one problem or operational area.
- **Pilot:** an optional limited release used when uncertainty or risk justifies it.
