# Positioning

## Purpose

This file owns AiA's current positioning. It does not own market proof, client counts, case studies, offer scope, or pricing.

AiA is positioned as:

- AI automation with a business and product perspective;
- a business-systems operator and consultant, not generic automation freelancing;
- diagnosis before implementation;
- reduced operational dependency on individual employees or the owner;
- conversion of relevant operational knowledge into structured business knowledge;
- practical return on investment;
- implementation with optional ongoing improvement;
- technology as a means, not the product.
