# Market and ICP

## Purpose

This file owns the current ideal-customer and market criteria. It does not own the unresolved initial market-wedge decision or GTM tactics.

- Primary geography: UAE.
- Target: SMEs and business operators with meaningful operational inefficiency or process complexity.
- Business size alone does not determine fit.
- A one-person business may be suitable when automation materially reduces workload or avoids unnecessary hiring.

## Practical fit signals

AiA is especially relevant where there is a meaningful operational problem or a concrete need to improve systems or processes. Practical fit signals include, as non-exclusive examples:

- high volumes of manual or repetitive work;
- weak or inconsistent follow-up;
- fragmented information or difficulty retrieving correct information quickly;
- excessive dependence on particular individuals, including business knowledge trapped in people;
- disconnected systems or fragmented workflows;
- repetitive customer or internal responses;
- weak operational visibility or control, including an owner losing control;
- a concrete need for a new business system;
- additional headcount being economically difficult or unjustified, where defined work could instead be handled by suitable automation, AI agents, or software;
- employing, or planning to employ, people primarily for repetitive work that could be reliably and safely automated;
- processes that need to run continuously, for long periods, or outside normal working hours, such as data collection or processing, monitoring, scraping, data preparation, or content-production workflows;
- a desire to use AI without a clear starting point.

These are fit signals, not mandatory ICP criteria. They do not mean that every such business needs AI, that an AI agent is automatically the right solution, that all repetitive human work should be automated, that any single workflow type defines AiA's service identity, or that continuous or 24/7 operation is guaranteed. AiA may address the need through process improvement, systemization, automation, integration, software, AI, or an appropriate combination.

Whether a specific Client or Project actually fits is evaluated in [[Assessment#Customer / Project Fit model]].

Real Estate is not a finalized canonical ICP. It is tracked only as a candidate in [[Market-Wedge]]. Any change to this boundary requires a Decision Record in [[DECISION-LOG]].
