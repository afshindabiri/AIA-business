# Focused Audit Offer

## Purpose

This file owns the current scope and value of the Focused Business Automation & AI Audit. It does not own current pricing or the internal Audit method.

The Focused Audit:

- covers one problem or one focused operational area;
- is diagnostic;
- identifies the real bottleneck or problem;
- determines what should and should not change;
- evaluates practical solution directions;
- recommends what should happen first;
- explains what implementation would involve;
- must be independently valuable even when no implementation follows.

The Client is buying clarity, not a large report.
