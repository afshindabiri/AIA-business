# Adopt the Customer / Project Fit Model

## Purpose

This record owns the rationale and decision snapshot for adopting AiA's cross-industry Customer / Project Fit model. It does not own the current model, an individual Fit evaluation, or a final Owner decision.

## Context

Industry, company size, employee count, current technology, and interest in AI are insufficient by themselves to determine whether a potential Client, Project, or Opportunity is a responsible and economically meaningful fit for AiA. Client suitability and Project suitability must remain conceptually distinguishable, while qualification stays lightweight and evidence-based.

## Decision

- Adopt a cross-industry Customer / Project Fit model owned by [[Assessment#Customer / Project Fit model]].
- Evaluate five core dimensions: Problem & Business Impact, Solution / Systemizability, Client Readiness, Delivery Feasibility, and Commercial / Economic Fit.
- Classify dimensions as `STRONG`, `MODERATE`, `WEAK`, or `UNKNOWN` without calculating an overall numeric score.
- Treat Strong Fit Signals as evidence, not mandatory gates; high-impact low-frequency opportunities remain eligible.
- Treat Knowledge Capture & Compounding Potential as a Strategic Value Multiplier, not a rejection gate or mandatory Business Second Brain output.
- Distinguish `UNKNOWN`, `REMEDIABLE`, and `MATERIAL / NON-REMEDIABLE` concerns before recommending.
- Use `RECOMMEND_ACCEPT`, `RECOMMEND_DECLINE`, or `NEEDS_MORE_INFORMATION` as advisory outcomes under the existing Owner-authority rule in [[Operating-Principles#AI recommendation and Owner decision authority]].

## Consequences

- Assessment can evaluate Fit consistently across industries without turning industry into the definition of Fit.
- A non-standard requirement is not rejected when AiA can responsibly deliver or source the needed capability; one-off work does not automatically become a standard Capability.
- Material missing evidence prevents a forced yes/no recommendation, and remediable weaknesses do not automatically cause decline.
- Severe material risk cannot be hidden by averaging otherwise strong dimensions.
- Fit reassessments remain distinguishable under existing current-versus-historical truth governance.
- No autonomous qualification engine, mandatory scoring system, new Offer, Solution, Product, folder structure, template, Industry-specific model, or Business Second Brain implementation is created.
