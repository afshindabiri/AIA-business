# Acquisition Strategy

## Purpose

This file owns the current acquisition-channel priorities. It does not own channel performance claims, sales stages, or pricing.

Current priorities:

1. Targeted outbound — primary initial engine.
2. Founder-led content.
3. Referrals and partners.
4. Selective UAE business communities and networking.

Do not wait for large social proof before selling. Build trust through:

- relevant problem diagnosis;
- working demonstrations;
- sample Audit material;
- transparent reasoning;
- founder expertise;
- examples where AI is not recommended.

Never fake Client work, testimonials, or results. No performance metric is assumed until evidence exists.
