# Referral and Partners

## Purpose

This file owns the current role of referrals and partners in acquisition. It does not own partner-specific agreements or assumed performance.

Use this channel for:

- trusted introductions;
- adjacent service partners;
- referral opportunities;
- selective ecosystem relationships.

Keep the approach simple. Do not invent partner programs, commissions, endorsement, exclusivity, or results without evidence. For each real relationship, record only confirmed context and link to the relevant Client record when appropriate.
