# Outbound Playbook

## Purpose

This draft file describes a high-level targeted-outbound approach. It does not own performance metrics, spam volume targets, or a Client's pipeline state.

## Outreach formula

```text
Relevant observation
+ Possible business problem
+ Value hypothesis
+ Low-friction CTA
```

- Never assert an unverified problem as fact.
- Use conditional language when the real process has not been observed.
- Lead with business relevance, not generic AI claims.
- The primary CTA concept is an Automation Opportunity Call.
- If evidence supports it, route to the [[Focused-Audit-Offer]].
- Record real relationship and engagement facts in the Client → Cycle → Phase path.
- Use [[Pipeline-Definition]] for stage meaning and [[Pricing-Policy]] for current commercial terms.

Quality and relevance take precedence over unsupported volume assumptions.
