# Focused Audit Method

## Purpose

This file owns the internal Focused Audit methodology. It does not own current commercial terms, a Client's quote/payment, or implementation delivery.

## Flow

```text
Scope
→ Understand
→ Evidence
→ Problem
→ Opportunity
→ Solution Options
→ Priority
→ Recommendation
```

## Questions answered

- What is happening?
- What is the actual problem?
- What is causing the bottleneck?
- What should change?
- What should not be done?
- What is the best approach?
- What should happen first?
- What would implementation involve?

## Opportunity outcomes

- `DO NOW`
- `PREPARE FIRST`
- `LATER`
- `DON'T DO`

Use evidence and judgment appropriate to the focused problem. Do not introduce numeric scoring or reproduce the old 1–5, 25-point, or percentage models.
