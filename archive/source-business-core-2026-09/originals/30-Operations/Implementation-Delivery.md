# Implementation Delivery

## Purpose

This file owns the high-level delivery gates for implementation. It does not own detailed engineering tasks, current pricing, or Client transaction values.

1. **READY TO BUILD** — scope, ownership, dependencies, and acceptance direction are sufficiently clear.
2. **READY TO TEST** — the implementation is complete enough for relevant verification.
3. **READY TO RELEASE / PILOT** — release conditions are met; choose a Pilot only when justified.
4. **READY FOR PRODUCTION** — production readiness and relevant operational controls are satisfied.
5. **READY FOR ACCEPTANCE** — agreed evidence and deliverables are ready for Client review.
6. **READY FOR HANDOVER** — acceptance, documentation, ownership, and transition requirements are satisfied.

AI, data, security, and other high-risk operational controls activate only when relevant. Do not recreate the old universal 27-section checklist or apply an exhaustive checklist to every engagement.

Related methods: [[Pilot]], [[Acceptance-and-Handover]], and [[Post-Implementation-Operations]]. Detailed engineering work remains outside Business Core.
