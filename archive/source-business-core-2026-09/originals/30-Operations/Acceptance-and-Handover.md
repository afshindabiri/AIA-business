# Acceptance and Handover

## Purpose

This file owns the current principles for explicit Acceptance and Handover. It does not own Client-specific acceptance facts or technical project artifacts.

## Acceptance

- Confirm the agreed scope was delivered.
- Confirm agreed success conditions were met or document known exceptions.
- Document material limitations.
- Record acceptance, open exceptions, or rejection in the Client Phase.
- Do not infer acceptance from silence.

## Handover

- Transfer only the relevant accounts and access.
- Provide relevant operating guidance and critical documentation.
- Confirm known limitations, ownership, responsibilities, and the support boundary.
- Link technical artifacts rather than duplicating them in Business Core.
- Make the transition to Client Takeover, [[Post-Implementation-Operations|System Care, or Continuous Improvement]] explicit.

Avoid unnecessary documentation.

These correspond to the final gates in [[Implementation-Delivery]].
