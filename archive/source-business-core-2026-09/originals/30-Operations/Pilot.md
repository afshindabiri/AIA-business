# Pilot

## Purpose

This file owns the conditions and outcome vocabulary for an optional Pilot. It does not make Pilots mandatory or own a specific Client's result.

Use a Pilot when there is meaningful:

- uncertainty;
- AI behavior risk;
- operational risk;
- an unproven process;
- substantial workflow change.

Simple deterministic integrations may skip a formal Pilot.

## Outcomes

- `SCALE`
- `IMPROVE`
- `PREPARE FIRST`
- `STOP`

Record the actual decision and evidence in the relevant Client Cycle or Phase. Delivery gates remain in [[Implementation-Delivery]].
